    Explore Our Rafting Adventures

    From relaxing family outings to challenging river adventures, discover an experience that matches your interests.

    Family River Adventure

    Enjoy a beginner-friendly rafting experience on gentle river sections. Learn basic paddling techniques, appreciate the surrounding scenery, and spend quality time with friends and family.

    • Duration: 2 hours
    • Difficulty: Beginner
    • Suitable for: Beginners and families

    White Water Challenge

    Experience a more energetic rafting adventure with stronger currents and exciting rapids. Work with your team, follow your guide's instructions, and enjoy an active day outdoors.

    • Duration: 4 hours
    • Difficulty: Intermediate
    • Suitable for: Adventurous participants

    Full-Day River Expedition

    Spend a full day exploring the river and its surrounding landscape. Enjoy a longer guided journey with opportunities to appreciate nature and share the experience with your group.

    • Duration: 6 hours
    • Difficulty: Advanced
    • Suitable for: Experienced participants
